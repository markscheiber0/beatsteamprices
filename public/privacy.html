Privacy policy

LiveComps. Effective November 23, 2026.

The short version. LiveComps has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. When you capture a card, the picture goes over an encrypted connection to the LiveComps server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude AI to read the card. We do not save or log the picture. The server keeps only a daily count of reads, tied to a random install code and your network address, so it can apply the free limit. Your scan log, settings and any optional price keys stay on your iPhone. You can erase everything the app stores from Settings with one button.

Who we are

LiveComps ("the app", "we") is made by Scheiber Intelligence, LLC. You can reach us at mark.scheiber@scheiberintelligence.com.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Optional keys. If you add a Poketrace or PokemonPriceTracker key, it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. It is never written into the app's files and we never receive it.
  • Install code. The first time you read a card, the app makes a random code and keeps it in the Keychain. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID or contacts. It lets the server count your free daily reads. Erase all my data leaves this code in place, so erasing cannot reset your free reads. It is removed when you delete the app and iOS clears its Keychain items.
  • Your scan log. Each card you look up is saved on your device with its name, set, number, Near Mint price and the time. You can delete entries in the app.
  • Your collection. If you tap Add to collection, the card, its condition, how many you own, the price you say you paid, and the card's prices at that moment are saved on your device. They are never sent to us. You can edit or delete them in the app.
  • Settings and counters. Things like your age group, whether you accepted the data notice, and how many requests a price service says you have left today.
  • Website data from the in-app browser. The app opens Whatnot in a web view. Like any browser, iOS stores cookies and sign-in data for that site on your device. We do not read your Whatnot password or messages.
  • Pictures. The app keeps the most recent video frame from the stream in memory so it is ready when you tap Capture. Frames are not saved and not sent anywhere unless you tap Capture. A photo taken with Camera scan is also held in memory only and is not saved to your Photos library.

What the LiveComps server receives

When you capture a card or take a card photo, the app sends one reduced-size picture to the LiveComps server, which runs on Cloudflare. The server uses it for one thing: it asks Anthropic's Claude AI to read the card name, set, number and finish, and sends that answer back to your phone.

  • The picture is held in memory while the request runs. We do not save it, log it, or use it to train anything. Request logging is turned off on the server.
  • Your install code and network address. The server counts reads per install code and per network address, for the free limit and to prevent abuse. These counts are deleted automatically after about a day and a half. We do not keep a history of your reads.
  • Subscription proof. If you subscribe, the app sends Apple's signed receipt for your subscription so the server can confirm it is active and give you the higher daily limit. The server checks Apple's signature. We do not store it, and we do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple handles the payment.
  • Cloudflare processes requests as our hosting provider and sees your network address as part of any internet request.

AI and accuracy

Card reading is done by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic). The server asks it only to identify a trading card in the picture and return the card details. It can make mistakes, so check every match yourself. Do not point the camera at people or anything other than a card.

What we do not do

  • We do not save or log your pictures, and we do not receive your scan log, searches or optional price keys.
  • We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not include analytics or advertising code in the app.

Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage statistics if you have chosen to share them in your iPhone settings. Those reports are anonymous and are handled by Apple.

Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Erase everything. Open Settings in the app, then Your data, then Erase all my data. This removes your saved keys, scan log, collection, settings and the Whatnot website data stored by the app, and takes you back to the first-time setup.
  • Delete scan log entries. Open Scan log and swipe left on a card to delete it.
  • Replace or remove a key. Open Settings and paste a new key, or use Erase all my data to remove all keys.
  • Data on our server. We keep no pictures and no account, so there is nothing to delete beyond the short-lived counts described above.
  • Before you delete the app. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted. Use Erase all my data first, then delete the app. The install code is also removed when iOS clears the app's Keychain items.
  • Data held by other companies. To end access, delete or regenerate your key in your account with that company. To ask about data they hold, contact them. Anthropic's retention is described above.

Children

LiveComps is for people 13 and older, and its livestream scanner is only for people 18 and older. When you first open the app, it asks for your date of birth to decide which features you can use. We do not save or send your birthday. Only your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) is saved, on your iPhone, and Erase all my data removes it. If you enter an age under 13, the app stops and does not collect card pictures or keys. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). People 13 to 17 can read cards with AI, which only identifies the card in the picture. We do not collect their name, contact details or location, and we do not use anyone's pictures for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will help.

Security

All connections from the app use HTTPS, and your optional keys and install code are kept in the iOS Keychain. The server limits how many reads each install and network can make per day. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device locked and treat your keys like passwords. If you think a key has been exposed, regenerate it with the provider.

Your rights

Because we do not keep your pictures or any account, there is almost nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you live somewhere with privacy rights, such as California or the European Economic Area, you can still contact us at mark.scheiber@scheiberintelligence.com with a request and we will respond.

Changes

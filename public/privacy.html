Privacy policy

LiveComps. Effective November 21, 2026.

The short version. LiveComps has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we do not run any servers that receive your data. Your keys, scan log and settings stay on your iPhone. When you capture a card, the app sends the picture or the card name to the services you set up with your own keys, and nothing else. You can erase everything the app stores from Settings with one button.

Who we are

LiveComps ("the app", "we") is made by Scheiber Intelligence, LLC. You can reach us at mark.scheiber@scheiberintelligence.com.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your keys. The keys you enter for Claude (Anthropic), Poketrace and PokemonPriceTracker are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are never written into the app's files and we never receive them.
  • Your scan log. Each card you look up is saved on your device with its name, set, number, Near Mint price and the time. You can delete entries in the app.
  • Settings and counters. Things like the AI model name, whether you accepted the data notice, and how many requests a price service says you have left today.
  • Website data from the in-app browser. The app opens Whatnot in a web view. Like any browser, iOS stores cookies and sign-in data for that site on your device. We do not read your Whatnot password or messages.
  • Pictures. The app keeps the most recent video frame from the stream in memory so it is ready when you tap Capture. Frames are not saved and not sent anywhere unless you tap Capture. A photo taken with Camera scan is also held in memory only and is not saved to your Photos library.

What we do not do

  • We do not run servers that receive your pictures, keys, scan log or searches.
  • We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not include analytics or advertising code in the app.

Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage statistics if you have chosen to share them in your iPhone settings. Those reports are anonymous and are handled by Apple.

Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Erase everything. Open Settings in the app, then Your data, then Erase all my data. This removes your saved keys, scan log, settings and the Whatnot website data stored by the app, and takes you back to the first-time setup.
  • Delete scan log entries. Open Scan log and swipe left on a card to delete it.
  • Replace or remove a key. Open Settings and paste a new key, or use Erase all my data to remove all keys.
  • Before you delete the app. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted. Use Erase all my data first, then delete the app.
  • Data held by other companies. To end access, delete or regenerate your key in your account with that company. To ask about data they hold, contact them. Anthropic's retention is described above.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Security

All connections from the app use HTTPS, and keys are kept in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device locked and treat your keys like passwords. If you think a key has been exposed, regenerate it with the provider.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you live somewhere with privacy rights, such as California or the European Economic Area, you can still contact us at mark.scheiber@scheiberintelligence.com with a request and we will respond.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its date, and we will ask for your agreement again in the app if the change affects where your data goes.

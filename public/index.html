LiveComps

A card live stream accurate pricing tool for iPhone.

LiveComps helps you look up Pokemon card prices while you watch a live stream. You add your own price service keys in the app, and they stay on your phone.

This site holds the app's privacy policy, terms of use and support page.

LiveComps is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by The Pokemon Company, Whatnot or any price service.

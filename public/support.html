Support

LiveComps. Effective November 23, 2026.

Need help with LiveComps? Email mark.scheiber@scheiberintelligence.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing.

How do I erase my data?

In the app, open Settings, then Your data, then Erase all my data. This removes your saved keys, scan log, collection, settings and Whatnot sign-in data stored by the app. A random install code stays so the free daily reads cannot be reset. Do this before you delete the app, because iOS can keep saved keys after an app is deleted. See the Privacy policy for details.

How many free card reads do I get?

20 AI card reads a day. The count shows under your result and in Settings, and resets daily. Manual lookup does not use reads. Erasing your data does not reset the count.

How do I get more reads?

Tap Get more reads, or open Settings, then LiveComps Plus. It is an optional monthly subscription with a higher daily limit.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel in your iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then LiveComps. Apple handles billing and refunds (reportaproblem.apple.com). If you reinstalled or changed phones, open LiveComps Plus in the app and tap Restore purchases.

How do I change or remove a price key?

Poketrace and PokemonPriceTracker keys are optional. Open Settings and paste a new key into the matching field. To remove keys completely, use Erase all my data. To stop a key working everywhere, delete or regenerate it in your account with the company that issued it.

Where do I get price keys?

Poketrace and PokemonPriceTracker each have a free plan with its own key. Links are in Settings.

The app says a price source is at its daily limit.

Free plans have a daily request limit. The app skips a source until its limit resets and uses the next one, then the free backup sources. Those backup sources only show a Near Mint price.

Why is the card or price wrong?

The AI can misread a card, and a price service can match a different version, such as a reverse holo or another set. Tap "Not right? Next match" to see other matches, and always check the card yourself before you bid.

Why does it say "est" next to a price?

Some sources only give a Near Mint price. In that case the Lightly Played, Heavily Played and Damaged numbers are estimates calculated from it. They are not real market prices.

Does the app record streams?

No. It reads one video frame in memory when you tap Capture and does not save it.

Does the app work with Japanese cards?

It tries. Japanese price data is limited, so a Japanese card may show a match with no price. The app warns you when it cannot find a Japanese price.

How do I erase my data?

In the app, open Settings, then Your data, then Erase all my data. This removes your saved keys, scan log, settings and Whatnot sign-in data stored by the app. Do this before you delete the app, because iOS can keep saved keys after an app is deleted. See the Privacy policy for details.

How do I change or remove a key?

Open Settings and paste a new key into the matching field. To remove keys completely, use Erase all my data. To stop a key working everywhere, delete or regenerate it in your account with the company that issued it.

Where do I get keys?

Claude keys come from the Anthropic Console. Poketrace and PokemonPriceTracker each have a free plan with its own key. Links are in Settings under Where to get keys.

The app says a price source is at its daily limit.

Free plans have a daily request limit. The app skips a source until its limit resets and uses the next one, then the free backup sources. Those backup sources only show a Near Mint price.

Why is the card or price wrong?

The AI can misread a card, and a price service can match a different version, such as a reverse holo or another set. Tap "Not right? Next match" to see other matches, and always check the card yourself before you bid.

Why does it say "est" next to a price?

Some sources only give a Near Mint price. In that case the Lightly Played, Heavily Played and Damaged numbers are estimates calculated from it. They are not real market prices.

Does the app record streams?

No. It reads one video frame in memory when you tap Capture and does not save it.

Does the app work with Japanese cards?

It tries. Japanese price data is limited, so a Japanese card may show a match with no price. The app warns you when it cannot find a Japanese price.
